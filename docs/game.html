Game Review: Space Hotel

I played Space Hotel and it’s a simple space management game. The graphics are basic, but it still feels like you’re running a space station. You don’t control a character, you just manage everything like food, oxygen, and tools. You build and expand the station, which is cool. There’s no real talking to characters, but you take care of guests. The game can be hard if you don’t plan well and run out of resources. The music fits the space theme. It also kind of jokes that you’ll fail a lot. It connects to real life with space travel, and I think it could be better with clearer instructions and fewer bugs.
